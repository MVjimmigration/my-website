Meet Mitika

Mitika Jain, RCIC

Mitika Jain is a Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant and founder of MVJ Immigration Services Ltd.

Before starting MVJ, Mitika spent over five years working at a Surrey-based nonprofit supporting temporary and migrant workers. Her experience included preparing applications such as open work permits for vulnerable individuals facing workplace challenges.

That experience shaped MVJ's approach: listen carefully, communicate clearly and treat every client's circumstances with respect.
